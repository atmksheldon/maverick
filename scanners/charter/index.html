MAVERICK research scanner

Host: scanner-fsn1.maverickbot.net · IPv4 178.105.116.36

This host is a slow, polite network scanner that fingerprints internet-connected robots (vacuums, humanoids, lawn bots, telepresence units, industrial arms) for a research project called MAVERICK. If you reached this page because something on your network received a probe from this IP, this page explains why.

What we do

  • Send slow Masscan SYN sweeps (~1000 packets per second) targeting a narrow set of robot-related ports (e.g. miIO UDP 54321, Tuya MQTT 1883/8883, Unitree SDK 8080/8081, ROS XML-RPC 11311).
  • Record only existence and fingerprint: that a device is reachable, what model it appears to be, and where it geolocates at city level only.

What we do not do

  • No exploitation. We never attempt to log in, send commands, or trigger device actions.
  • No contents. We never log floorplans, camera frames, audio, telemetry payloads, credentials, or personally identifying information.
  • No street-level geolocation. Records are reverse-geocoded to city level and no finer.
